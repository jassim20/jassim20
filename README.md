# Electronics & Embedded Systems Engineering

**PCB Design | Embedded Systems | Hardware Development**

## About Me

My engineering interests focus on electronic circuit design, microcontroller-based systems, PCB development, and hardware reverse engineering.

This portfolio documents engineering projects from initial design and schematic development to implementation, verification, and technical documentation.
